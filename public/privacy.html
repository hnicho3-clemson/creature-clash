Privacy policy

What we keep, and why

Last updated: October 23, 2026

The short version: you can play Creature Clash as a guest without telling us anything. If you make an account, we keep a username, a scrambled password, one email address and your game progress. Players can't chat or see each other. We don't show ads, sell data, or use tracking cookies. The game counts anonymous totals, like how many matches were played today, and reports crashes so we can fix them.

Playing as a guest

Guest progress (creatures, DNA, parts and settings) is saved only in your own browser. It never reaches our servers. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.

What an account stores

  • Username. Chosen by the player. We ask players not to use their real name.
  • Password, stored only as a scrambled (hashed) value. We can't read it.
  • One email address. For players 13 and older, their own. For players under 13, a parent's or guardian's. We use it only to confirm the account, approve it, and reset the password.
  • Whether the player is under 13. We ask for a birth year at sign-up, use it once, and don't store it.
  • Game progress: creatures, levels, XP, DNA, parts, challenge results and settings.
  • Security records: sign-in sessions and short-lived counters that slow down password guessing. The counters store scrambled values, not IP addresses, and are deleted within a day.

Children under 13

We follow the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). When a player under 13 signs up, we email the parent or guardian they name. The account doesn't work until that adult approves it. If nobody approves within 7 days, we delete the request, including the email address.

A parent or guardian can at any time:

  • see what we store about their child,
  • delete the account and all its data (from the Account page, or by contacting us),
  • refuse further collection, which means deleting the account.

Children's accounts have no chat, messaging, public profiles or way to share personal information with other players.

Game statistics and error reports

To see how the game is doing and to fix bugs, the game sends two kinds of anonymous information to our own server. Nothing goes to an outside analytics or advertising company, and no cookie or ID is used, so we can't tell which player or browser anything came from.

  • Daily counts: how many times the game was opened, how many browsers opened it that day, how many matches and challenges started, and the total minutes played. Each day is stored as a few totals, nothing per player. To count a browser only once a day, the game saves today's date in that browser. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, these counts are skipped.
  • Error reports: when the game crashes, the error message and the place in our code where it happened. Email addresses, codes and long numbers are removed before it's sent. Reports are grouped by error and deleted once the bug is fixed, or after 90 days at most.

Cookies

Signed-in players get one cookie, cc_session, which keeps them signed in. It's required for accounts to work. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

Who helps us run the game

We don't sell or share personal information. These services process it only to run Creature Clash:

  • Vercel hosts the website and game.
  • Neon hosts the database.
  • GitHub stores the encrypted daily database backups. It can't read them.
  • Resend sends account emails.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr serve fonts and the 3D engine. They see your IP address when your browser downloads those files, as with any website.

How long we keep things

  • Accounts: until you delete them. Deleting removes the account, progress, sessions and codes right away.
  • Unapproved sign-ups: 7 days at most.
  • Reset and confirmation codes: 20 minutes. Parent approval links: 7 days.
  • Backups: the database is copied once a day into an encrypted backup that's kept for 30 days, so deleted data can stay in a backup for up to 30 days before it expires. Our database host also keeps a short restore history of its own.
  • Game statistics: daily totals with no personal information, kept as long as they're useful. Error reports: 90 days at most.

Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sign-in uses secure, HTTP-only cookies over HTTPS. Codes are single-use and expire. Repeated guesses are blocked. No system is perfect, but we work to keep your information safe.
