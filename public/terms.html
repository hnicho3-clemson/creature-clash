The game rules

Last updated: October 23, 2026

By playing Creature Clash or making an account, you agree to these rules. If you're under 13, a parent or guardian has to agree for you.

Playing fair

  • Pick a username that's kind and isn't your real name, an email address, or a phone number.
  • Don't try to break, overload or trick the game or its servers, and don't use tools that do.
  • Don't sign in to someone else's account.
  • We can remove usernames that break these rules, and close accounts that keep breaking them.

Your account

  • Keep your password secret. If you think someone knows it, change it with "Forgot password?".
  • You can delete your account any time from the Account page.

DNA, parts and progress

DNA, XP and parts are only for playing the game. They have no real-money value and can't be bought, sold or traded. We may change game balance, prices and rewards in updates, and we may fix progress that came from bugs or cheating.

The game itself

Creature Clash is provided as it is, and we're still building it. We'll try to keep it running and your progress safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or bug-free. Guest progress lives in your browser, so clearing site data deletes it.

Changes

If we change these rules, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also show up in the game.
