Guide

How to play

  1. Build a creature. Stick legs, jaws, horns and more onto it in the Workshop. Every part changes how it moves and fights.
  2. Pick it in the build phase. You have 2 minutes, then the battle starts.
  3. Crack the enemy nests. Walk down a lane and attack the glowing egg nests. Break them all to win!
  4. Stay alive. You only get one life per match. Teammates can revive you if you get knocked down.
  5. Earn DNA. Beating enemy creatures gives DNA, which buys new parts.
